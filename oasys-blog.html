Field notes from the projects we support

Updates, milestones and perspectives from OASYS missions across Africa, Asia, Europe and the Caribbean.

10.02.2026

OASYS presents its innovative digital solution (OBIS) at the MSHP DU

Digital

As part of its mission to provide technical and fiduciary assistance to the Delivery Unit of the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene (DU-MSHP), OASYS has deployed its OBIS (OASYS Business Information System) platform, a digital solution designed to optimize, secure and streamline management and collaboration processes between OASYS and the DU-MSHP.

Developed using OASYS's in-house expertise, OBIS enables the digitization of key procedures, the operational and financial monitoring of activities, and improved traceability of exchanges and decisions. The goal is to enhance organizational efficiency, reduce processing times, and improve the quality of interactions between the two entities.

During a dedicated session, OASYS provided an in-depth demonstration of the platform to the DU-MSHP team, highlighting its features, ease of use, and tangible benefits in terms of performance, transparency, and management. This presentation generated significant interest from DU-MSHP, which praised the innovative initiative as aligned with the high standards of quality and excellence expected in technical assistance.

Through OBIS, OASYS illustrates its ability to design tailor-made digital solutions, adapted to the specific needs of technical and financial partners (TFPs) and public institutions and integrated into a logic of continuous improvement of services and creation of sustainable added value in its offering of consulting and technical assistance services.
10.02.2026

Strategic meeting between OASYS and the Gates Foundation regarding technical assistance to the Delivery Unit (DU) of the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene in Senegal

Governance

OASYS hosted a delegation from the Gates Foundation as part of a partnership focused on providing technical and fiduciary assistance to the Delivery Unit (DU) of the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene. This meeting was part of the funding provided by the Gates Foundation to strengthen the organizational and operational capacities of the DU-MSHP in the context of reduced foreign funding and a transition towards endogenous financing of the health system.

During the discussions, OASYS presented an overview of the technical assistance mission, the results achieved to date, and the methodological approach adopted to support the Delivery Unit in achieving its strategic objectives. As a technical and fiduciary agency, OASYS contributes to the scaling up of the DU's functionality and capacity (mobilization and deployment of experts/consultants) and ensures the rigorous management of the financial resources made available, in compliance with transparency and accountability requirements.

The Gates Foundation delegation expressed its satisfaction with the mission's execution, praising the quality of the leadership, the relevance of the tools implemented, and the added value OASYS brought to the Delivery Unit's daily operations. This recognition reinforces OASYS's credibility as a trusted partner for international donors and public institutions.

This meeting also helped to consolidate the common vision around the continuation of support, with an emphasis on the sustainability of achievements, the strengthening of internal capacities and the continuous improvement of the institutional performance of the DU.
10.02.2026

Supervision of the deployment of the M-Vaccine application in 49 health districts in Ivory Coast

Digital

As part of the Gavi-funded mVaccine application expansion project, in collaboration with Orange Côte d'Ivoire as the technology partner, the OASYS-CHAI Côte d'Ivoire consortium is providing technical assistance to the Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI), the beneficiary. The OASYS-CHAI Côte d'Ivoire consortium played a strategic role in overseeing and supporting the deployment of the mVaccine solution in 49 health districts across Côte d'Ivoire.

This key phase of the project strengthened healthcare workers' adoption of the digital tool through training sessions, close support, and formative supervision in the field. The OASYS – CHAI CI consortium teams worked in close coordination with regional and departmental institutional stakeholders (DRS, DDS) to ensure the quality of deployments, the availability of equipment, and methodological consistency in the application's use.

Beyond the technical deployment, OASYS contributed to the continuous improvement of the system by formulating operational recommendations based on field missions, particularly regarding digital logistics, connectivity, and the organization of post-deployment monitoring. This integrated approach helped consolidate the effective use of M-Vaccin as a decision-making tool and for monitoring the performance of the vaccination system.

Through this mission, OASYS confirms its position as a major player in the digitalization of health systems in Africa, capable of supporting large-scale projects with high strategic and institutional value.
10.02.2026

OASYS is supporting the Guinean Ministry of Health by training data collection agents for the national study on medical procedure pricing.

Health & Social

As part of the mission conducted on behalf of the Bureau of Strategy and Development (BSD) of the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene of Guinea, with financial support from the World Bank, OASYS provided training to 45 collection agents and supervisors as part of phase 1 of the national study on the costs and pricing of medical, biological and medical imaging procedures in Guinea.

This strategic mission strengthened the investigators' capacity in cost calculation methodology, the use of data collection tools, and the data quality requirements expected to guide health financing reforms. OASYS also provided field supervision, data consolidation, and ongoing technical support to the teams during the data collection phase.

Through its recognized expertise in health economics, health systems organization and technical assistance, OASYS actively contributes to the production of reliable data, essential for informed and evidence-based decision-making, in service of equitable access to quality care in Guinea.
