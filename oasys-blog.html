OASYS presents its innovative digital solution (OBIS) at the MSHP DU

As part of its mission to provide technical and fiduciary assistance to the Delivery Unit of the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene (DU-MSHP), OASYS has deployed its OBIS (OASYS Business Information System) platform.

Strategic meeting between OASYS and the Gates Foundation regarding technical assistance to the Delivery Unit (DU) of the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene in Senegal

Supervision of the deployment of the M-Vaccine application in 49 health districts in Ivory Coast

As part of the Gavi-funded mVaccine application expansion project, the OASYS-CHAI Côte d'Ivoire consortium oversaw the deployment of the mVaccine solution in 49 health districts across Côte d'Ivoire.

OASYS is supporting the Guinean Ministry of Health by training data collection agents for the national study on medical procedure pricing.

As part of the mission conducted on behalf of the Bureau of Strategy and Development (BSD) of the Ministry of Health of Guinea, with support from the World Bank, OASYS trained 45 collection agents and supervisors.
